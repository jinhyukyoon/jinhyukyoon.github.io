Science Communication

Public speech and on-stage content

My role: speaker, actor, content development

Science communication, a core program of the Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science & Creativity (KOFAC), presents science to the public in an easy way. I developed and performed content such as talks, street performances, and plays. I mainly gave public talks on robotics, bioengineering, control, machine learning, and science communication at middle schools, high schools, libraries, and science institutions. Audiences ranged from elementary and middle school students to office workers, teachers, and the general public. Talks combined infographics, videos, stories, and audience participation through games and science experiments. Audience surveys showed the talks were easy to understand even though they covered much unfamiliar and complex science. The talks are listed below.
